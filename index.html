Healthcare Database for Patient Flow Optimization

The Problem

Hospitals often struggle with fragmented patient flow data, making it harder to evaluate length of stay, staff workload, resource usage, and visit patterns.

Why It Mattered

Without structured data, leaders may lack timely visibility into operational bottlenecks that affect throughput, staffing, and patient care efficiency.

The Solution

Designed a relational healthcare database with normalized tables, primary and foreign keys, SQL queries, and analytics-ready reporting outputs.

The Workflow
  • Created ERD and database schema
  • Built patient, visit, staff, and resource tables
  • Wrote SQL queries for operational analysis
  • Generated visual outputs for portfolio documentation
Measurable Outcomes

Produced outputs showing average length of stay, staff workload distribution, visit summaries, and resource utilization duration.

Leadership / Business Impact

Shows how database design and SQL analytics can support hospital operations, staffing decisions, workflow improvement, and executive reporting.

Military Water Consumption Dashboard

The Problem

Field operations require accurate water planning, but estimates can become inconsistent when based on rough calculations or informal notes.

Why It Mattered

Water planning affects soldier safety, logistics, sustainment, readiness, and command-level decision-making during field operations.

The Solution

Created a planning model to estimate water requirements based on soldier count, mission duration, and temperature conditions.

The Workflow
  • Built consumption assumptions by weather category
  • Calculated liters and gallons required
  • Structured the data for leadership reporting
  • Developed a reusable planning framework
Measurable Outcomes

Supported planning scenarios for hundreds of soldiers across multiple days and different environmental conditions.

Leadership / Business Impact

Demonstrates logistics planning, data-informed leadership, and the ability to convert field requirements into decision-support tools.
