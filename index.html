Healthcare Database for Patient Flow Optimization

The Problem

Hospitals often struggle with fragmented patient flow data, making it difficult to evaluate length of stay, staff workload, resource usage, and visit patterns.

Why It Mattered

Without structured data, leaders may lack timely visibility into bottlenecks that affect efficiency, patient throughput, and operational planning.

The Solution

Designed a relational healthcare database using normalized tables, primary and foreign keys, SQL queries, and analytics-ready reporting outputs.

Workflow
  • Created ERD and database schema
  • Built patient, visit, staff, and resource tables
  • Wrote SQL queries for operational analytics
  • Generated screenshots for portfolio documentation
Measurable Outcomes

Produced query outputs showing average length of stay, staff workload distribution, visit summaries, and resource utilization patterns.

Leadership / Business Impact

Demonstrates how database design and SQL analytics can support hospital operations, staffing decisions, and process improvement.

Military Water Consumption Dashboard

The Problem

Field operations require accurate water planning, but estimates can be inconsistent when based on rough calculations or informal notes.

Why It Mattered

Water planning affects soldier safety, logistics, sustainment, and command-level readiness during training or operational missions.

The Solution

Created a planning model to estimate water requirements based on soldier count, duration, and temperature conditions.

Workflow
  • Built consumption assumptions by weather category
  • Calculated liters and gallons required
  • Structured the data for leadership reporting
  • Developed a reusable planning framework
Measurable Outcomes

Supported planning scenarios for hundreds of soldiers across multiple days and environmental conditions.

Leadership / Business Impact

Demonstrates logistics planning, data-informed leadership, and the ability to convert operational requirements into clear decision-support tools.
